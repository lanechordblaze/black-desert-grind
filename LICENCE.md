## 🧩 About

**Black Desert Grind Helper** is a desktop overlay for Black Desert on PC featuring an auto-fishing loop, AFK training timer, grind spot tracker, contribution point planner, inventory weight monitor, and enhance stack counter.

Based on community tools like **BDO Grind Tracker**, **Black Desert Overlay**, and **Arsha Helper**.

---

## ✨ Features

### ⚔️ Core Grind Tools
- Auto-Fishing Loop — re-cast and loot on a timed cycle
- AFK Training Timer — track black spirit training windows
- Grind Spot Tracker — log monsters killed per rotation and silver per hour
- Enhancement Stack Counter — log failstacks and cron stone usage
- Contribution Point Planner — map out node and worker investments

### 🎒 Inventory & Economy
- Inventory Weight Monitor — warn before you cap out mid-farm
- Marketplace Price Watcher — pin item prices to the overlay
- Silver per Hour Estimator — quick profit readout per session
- Repair & Durability Alerts — know when your gear needs a trip back

### 🏋️ Session & Training
- Session Timer — track play windows and break reminders
- Skill Cooldown Layout — arrange Awakening and Succession cooldowns
- Buff Uptime Tracker — follow meal, elixir, and scroll timers
- Loot Filter Notes — keep a per-spot drop reference panel
